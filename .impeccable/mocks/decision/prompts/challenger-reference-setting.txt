Desktop web landing page for PostulaDo, a Spanish-language tool that turns a job posting URL into a structured evaluation of that posting against your profile plus three tailored documents. Rendered edge to edge, crisp readable text, no browser window, no device frame, no desk, no hands, no people. The reference images are style inspiration adapted into a real working product; their layout and content are not to be copied.

The visitor is a professional in active job search. The dominant move is a two-column spread: the left column carries the posting being evaluated, labeled EJEMPLO, with the title "Senior Backend Engineer - Aurelia Fintech" at https://example.com/jobs/nestjs-dev and its requirements set as a dense list - TypeScript, NestJS, PostgreSQL, Redis, Docker, Kubernetes, AWS, 3 anos de experiencia, and responsibilities including "Desarrollar APIs con NestJS y bases de datos relacionales". The right column carries the candidate's profile and the three artifacts produced from it, headed ANALISIS DE ENFOQUE, CARTA DE PRESENTACION and CV OPTIMIZADO, each with two readable lines of Spanish text. Between the columns runs a vertical centre rail of small letter-keys A through R, some of them marked where a requirement meets the profile.

A wide annotation margin runs down the outer edge of the spread, headed NOTAS DEL EVALUADOR, with two short handwritten notes and one small red-rubric stamp impression. In the margin sits a single input field labeled OFERTA (URL) holding the example URL, followed by a solid filled action button labeled EVALUAR.

The frame carries the PostulaDo wordmark top left, a keyed navigation list in the outer margin reading PRODUCTO, COMO FUNCIONA, PRECIOS, and a right-aligned outlined ENTRAR button.

Visual law: unbleached bone paper as the ground, iron-black ink for all type, and exactly one rubric vermilion used for the centre rail keys, the rules and one stamp. Type is a transitional serif for the display and body, with a small-caps pearl face for the apparatus and labels. Every rule, bracket and crosshair is a hairline at one device pixel. The signature is the centre rail with its letter-keys. No gradients, no glass, no rounded pills, no icons, no sparkles, no chat bubbles.
