Desktop web landing page for PostulaDo, a Spanish-language tool that turns a job posting URL into a completed application package. Rendered edge to edge across the full width, crisp readable text, no browser window, no device frame, no desk, no hands, no people.

The visitor is a professional in active job search. The page must show that the tool completes the tedious form for you: a preprinted triplicate employment application form, seen flat and filling most of the viewport, its fields already written in typewriter characters. The form is clearly labeled EJEMPLO and evaluates a demonstration posting titled "Senior Backend Engineer - Aurelia Fintech" at https://example.com/jobs/nestjs-dev.

The form's own anatomy carries the screen: a top block with the applicant header - POSTULANTE, CORREO, TELEFONO - filled in as values; a section DATOS DE LA OFERTA with a field labeled URL DE LA OFERTA holding the example URL and the offer title and company written beside it; a ruled table EXPERIENCIA LABORAL with three filled rows, years, company and role in aligned columns; a section REQUISITOS SOLICITADOS listing TypeScript, NestJS, PostgreSQL, Redis, Docker and 3 anos de experiencia as written entries. A perforation edge runs down the left margin, and a violet-blue carbon copy of the same form peeks out from beneath the top sheet, offset, showing the same entries transferred to the second copy.

Three slips are stapled to the top-right of the form, titled ANALISIS DE ENFOQUE, CARTA DE PRESENTACION and CV OPTIMIZADO, each with two readable lines of Spanish text. A bold filled action button labeled EVALUAR sits at the end of the URL field.

The frame carries the PostulaDo wordmark top left, a slim top navigation reading PRODUCTO, COMO FUNCIONA, PRECIOS, and a right-aligned outlined ENTRAR button.

Visual law: cold pale bond paper as the ground, one carbon-copy violet-blue for the duplicate and the typed values, graphite-black ink for type and rules, and one restrained brick-red for the ORIGINAL copy mark and the filled action button. Two type voices only: a condensed grotesque in spaced capitals for the form's section labels, and a monospaced typewriter face for every written value, as if keyed by a machine. The signature is the carbon copy itself - the same entries visible twice, offset, on two sheets. No gradients, no glass, no rounded pills, no icons, no sparkles, no chat bubbles.
