Desktop web landing page for PostulaDo, a Spanish-language tool that turns a job posting URL into a structured evaluation of that posting against your profile plus three tailored documents. Rendered edge to edge across the full width, crisp readable text, no browser window, no device frame, no desk, no hands, no people.

The visitor is a professional in active job search. In one screen they must see that this is not a chatbot: one job posting is broken down into its requirements, compared against their profile line by line, and yields three separate artifacts. The dominant object is a printed evaluation sheet filling most of the viewport, clearly labeled EJEMPLO, evaluating a demonstration posting titled "Senior Backend Engineer - Aurelia Fintech" at https://example.com/jobs/nestjs-dev. Down the left of the sheet runs a column of that posting's requirements in real content: TypeScript, NestJS, PostgreSQL, Redis, Docker, Kubernetes, AWS, 3 anos de experiencia, and responsibilities including "Desarrollar APIs con NestJS y bases de datos relacionales" and "Configurar y desplegar contenedores con Docker". A second column carries the candidate's evidence. Several cells across the sheet carry a broad fluorescent highlighter swipe where a requirement meets the profile. Three slips hang from the sheet, titled ANALISIS DE ENFOQUE, CARTA DE PRESENTACION and CV OPTIMIZADO, each with two readable lines of Spanish text.

In the sheet's header block, one input field labeled OFERTA (URL) holds the example URL, followed by a solid filled action button labeled EVALUAR. Along the right edge of the sheet runs a narrow margin column headed NOTAS DEL EVALUADOR with two handwritten marginal notes, and one oxblood rubber-stamped impression sits over a single row.

The frame carries the PostulaDo wordmark top left, a slim top navigation reading PRODUCTO, COMO FUNCIONA, PRECIOS, and a right-aligned outlined ENTRAR button; the sheet fills the rest of the viewport and has its own form header and a footer rule.

Visual law: cold pale grey-blue forms paper as the ground; graphite-black ink for all type and every hairline rule; one fluorescent chartreuse highlighter colour used only for the match swipes; one oxblood-red stamp impression used once. Two type voices only: a condensed grotesque in spaced capitals for the form's field labels and headers, and a monospaced typewriter face for values and small annotations. The signature is the highlighter swipe crossing a rubric row, its edge ragged and letting the rule beneath show through. No gradients, no glass, no rounded pills, no icons, no sparkles, no chat bubbles.
