Desktop web landing page for PostulaDo, a Spanish-language tool that turns a job posting URL into a structured evaluation and three tailored documents. Rendered edge to edge, crisp readable text, no browser window, no device frame, no desk, no hands, no people. The reference images are style inspiration adapted into a real working product; their layout and content are not to be copied.

The visitor is a professional in active job search and must see in one screen that one posting becomes several coherent artifacts. The dominant move is a programme sheet. A numbered list of your applications runs down the page like tonight's programme, each entry a number in a heavy tilted block followed by a title, a company, a time and a state. The first entries read "Senior Backend Engineer - Aurelia Fintech", "Frontend Engineer - Kobalto" and "Data Analyst - Taller Norte", each with a state of ENVIADO, ENTREVISTA, OFERTA or RECHAZADO; the resolved ones are struck through with a hard line. At the head of the list one entry is expanded to show its extracted requirements: TypeScript, NestJS, PostgreSQL, Redis, Docker, 3 anos de experiencia.

Beside the programme, a single input field labeled OFERTA (URL) holds https://example.com/jobs/nestjs-dev and a solid filled action button labeled EVALUAR. Below, three panels titled ANALISIS DE ENFOQUE, CARTA DE PRESENTACION and CV OPTIMIZADO, each with two readable lines of Spanish text.

The frame carries the PostulaDo wordmark top left, a top navigation reading PRODUCTO, COMO FUNCIONA, PRECIOS, and a right-aligned filled ENTRAR button; a footer band carries the three panel titles and a line of small print.

Visual law: exactly two inks, an acid yellow and a deep black, with no midtone anywhere; every edge is hard and every shape is lit or unlit. Headlines are set in a heavy expressionist condensed face that leans hard off the vertical. The signature is the strike-through on a resolved programme entry. No gradients, no soft shadows, no grey, no rounded pills, no icons, no sparkles, no chat bubbles.
